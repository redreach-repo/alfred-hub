Essential phrases for everyday French in Canada

🙋 Greetings & Introductions

Bonjour !
Hello / Good day!
Use anytime — morning, afternoon, evening. Never wrong.
Je m'appelle Alfred.
My name is Alfred.
Literally: "I call myself Alfred"
Enchanté !
Nice to meet you!
Standard polite greeting when introduced
Comment allez-vous ?
How are you? (formal)
Use this in job interviews and professional settings
Ça va bien, merci !
I'm doing well, thank you!
Standard friendly reply

💼 Work & Professional (Calgary context)

Je cherche du travail.
I am looking for work.
Key phrase for job hunting in Alberta
J'ai de l'expérience en gestion.
I have experience in management.
Your main selling point in interviews
Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?
Could you repeat that, please?
Essential for TEF Speaking section
Pouvez-vous parler plus lentement ?
Can you speak more slowly?
Use freely — never be embarrassed to ask

🏠 Daily Life in Calgary

Je voudrais louer un appartement.
I would like to rent an apartment.
Key phrase for housing in Calgary
Combien ça coûte ?
How much does it cost?
Universal phrase for stores, services, everywhere
Il fait très froid aujourd'hui !
It's very cold today!
You WILL use this every Calgary winter 😄
C'est le vent chinook !
It's the chinook wind!
Alberta-specific warm wind — locals will love this

📝 TEF Canada Exam Phrases

À mon avis...
In my opinion...
Start TEF Writing/Speaking answers with this
D'une part... d'autre part...
On one hand... on the other hand...
Shows sophisticated argumentation — examiners love it
En conclusion...
In conclusion...
End your TEF written responses with this
Cependant / Néanmoins
However / Nevertheless
Linking words that elevate your writing score

📌 TEF Canada vs DELF/DALF

TEF Canada is specifically for immigration — only TEF Canada and TCF Canada are accepted for Express Entry. Book through Alliance Française or authorized centres in Dubai or online.

📌 CLB 7 Score Requirements

Approximately: Reading: 207–232/300 · Listening: 145–180/360 · Writing: 14–15/20 · Speaking: 14–15/20. Each section is scored independently.
